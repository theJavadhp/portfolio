Designing for retail investors at Mofid

  • Mofid
  • UI/UX Designer, 2016–2023
  • Graphic Designer, 2013–2016
  • Online brokerage & trading platform
Problem
The broker's account creation flow was friction-heavy and visually inconsistent with the rest of the platform, hurting completion, and its login wasn't mobile-friendly just as customers were moving from laptops to phones.
Approach
As lead designer of a three-person design group in a team of ten, took the authentication experience from concept to final — refined typography hierarchy and interaction patterns, and reduced the steps to first usable account state — then built the front end of the new responsive OAuth sign-in myself. The mobile account-opening flow covers ID check, signature upload, video verification, and an e-contract. Documented patterns for adoption across the broader platform.
Outcome
Patterns from this work were adopted across the platform, and the sign-in is still in use. By late 2022, 91% of visits to the sign-up site (about 490,000 a month) and 87% of My Mofid's 2.1 million monthly visits came from phones. Over six years on the software teams, after almost four years as Mofid's graphic designer, I designed web and mobile interfaces for the brokerage, including its website, the My Mofid customer portal, internal admin tools, and work on EasyTrader and the Pellekan loyalty program.
