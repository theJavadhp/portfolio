TrimoStudio — from a brief to a finished short video, step by step

  • Trimo Tech
  • Oct – Nov 2026
  • Built on OpenMontage (open source)
Problem
The goal was 30–60 second vertical videos — infographic scenes animated in code mixed with AI-generated clips — made from a brief, a chosen style and a reference. OpenMontage, the open-source agentic video system underneath, can produce a finished video, but it's driven from a coding-agent chat. And once the first Studio features were in, the web interface had two headers on every page, one destination under several names, five button styles, and no view of where a video was in its journey.
Approach
Built the Studio layer on top of OpenMontage: a New video page that takes a brief or pulls its content from a web page, a gallery of twenty styles (most with code-rendered previews), and a KIE connector for images, clips, narration and music. Each video then moves through steps — brief, story, narration, pictures, clips, build, render — and pictures are approved before any are paid for as animated clips. Later I added an autopilot that makes a whole video by itself inside a spending cap, shown as a chat assistant docked beside the video, and a hand-off that sends a finished reel straight to A2MATE.
Decision
Rebuilt the interface around one global bar, one project header and one stepper, so you always know which video you're in, which step it's at, and what to do next. Each step shows its content and its actions in one place, with one vocabulary and one button system. It stayed plain ES modules and CSS — no framework and no build step added.
Outcome
The frames on this page come from Trimotech videos made with it, in five of its styles. I made 249 commits on top of the open-source base between October 7 and November 19, 2026.
